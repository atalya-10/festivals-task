Part 2: Festival Advertisements

Read about three different festivals around the world

Reading 2
1 🇮🇹

The Eurochocolate Festival

Italy

Come to the international Eurochocolate Festival to celebrate your love of chocolate!

  • • Visit the town of Perugia, and see an amazing festival.
  • • Enjoy a variety of events and activities, including chocolate sculpture competitions.
  • • Taste different kinds of delicious chocolate.
  • • Take a tour of the famous Perugia Chocolate Factory and Museum.
2 🇹🇭

The Monkey Festival

Thailand

Don't miss this amazing cultural event!

  • • Located in the beautiful city of Lopburi.
  • • People dress up as monkeys and dance.
  • • Early in the morning, organizers put lots of fruit on long tables.
  • • Thousands of monkeys jump on the tables and eat the fruit!
3 🇮🇱

The Haifa Film Festival

Israel

Do you like drama? Do you prefer comedy? You can combine both at the Haifa Film Festival!

  • • See the best films, like comedies, dramas and musicals.
  • • Meet the people who make the films.
  • • Learn about people and places from around the world.
  • • Go on a tour of Haifa, alone or with a tour guide.
